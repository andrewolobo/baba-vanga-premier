The week in one read

Three of the four divisions open the week with a favourite priced far shorter than the numbers justify. Arsenal at home to Coventry is the clearest of them: the market has it as a formality, our model has it as a two-goal win with a live chance of a nervy first half. We take the win, not the handicap.

Further down, the National League is where the edge sits. Promoted sides are being underrated by roughly a third of a goal per match, and two of this weekend's away picks come straight out of that gap. Expect low scores and expect them to hold.

The one to leave alone is Brentford against Tottenham. Confidence sits at 52% for a reason — the shape of that game changes entirely on whether Spurs press high, and nothing in the last six matches tells us which way it falls.
